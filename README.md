## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer with 3+ years of experience** building backend services, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and AI-powered applications.

My work focuses on converting complex workflows into **reliable, scalable, observable, and maintainable systems**. I enjoy working across the complete engineering lifecycle—from understanding a problem and designing the architecture to implementing, deploying, monitoring, and improving the system.

- ⚙️ Building backend services using **Go, Java, Python, REST, and gRPC**
- ☁️ Developing cloud-native systems across **GCP, AWS, Kubernetes, and Terraform**
- 📨 Working with event-driven architectures using **Pub/Sub and Apache Kafka**
- 🤖 Building applied AI solutions with **LLM agents, RAG, embeddings, and vector search**
- 📊 Improving production reliability through **monitoring, tracing, and automation**
- 🧠 Interested in distributed systems, developer infrastructure, and AI engineering
